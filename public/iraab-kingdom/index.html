    How the kingdom works

    Travelers, not citizens

    “The same traveler, a new errand, a new gate.”

    Townsfolk are travelers. Their shape (Ism Mufrad, Al-Muthannā…) is a family name — fixed. Which castle they enter depends on today’s errand (the ʿāmil / road sign):

    Rafʿ

    Doing the action / starting the sentence

    Naṣb

    Receiving the action

    Khafḍ

    Preposition or possession

    Jazm

    Verbs only — jazm particle

    The castle stamps them Aṣliyyah or Farʿiyyah for the visit. The seal isn’t kept — issued fresh at each gate. So كِتَابٌ isn’t a resident of Rafʿ; it’s Ism Mufrad visiting Rafʿ, Naṣb, or Khafḍ depending on the day’s errand.
